# PRD: Portfolio

## Goal
A fast, dark, editorial portfolio that shows Devendra's backend-first full-stack work to recruiters at startups and product companies in India, and builds a personal brand for future freelancing.

## Audience
1. Recruiters and hiring managers: skim for about 30 seconds and click what is clickable.
2. Engineers reviewing depth: case studies and repos.
3. Potential freelance clients.

## Page order
Hero, About, Case studies (Dropty), Selected work, Skills, Experience and education, Contact.
Navbar: Work, About, Skills, Experience, Contact, plus a Resume button.

## In scope
Static pages, MDX case studies, typed data files, subtle animations with a "Motion off" toggle, contact section with an email link and an optional form, SEO basics, accessibility basics.

## Out of scope for now
Database, auth, blog, services section, testimonials, light mode, Cineflix project, analytics.

## Success criteria
- A recruiter understands role, stack and best work within the first screens.
- Every project card and case study answers: what problem, what I built, with which tech, what was my part.
- No major Lighthouse red flags, keyboard-only navigation works, no horizontal overflow at 360 px.
- No invented facts. The resume is the source of truth.
